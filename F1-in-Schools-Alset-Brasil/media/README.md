# 📸 Project Media: Industrial Fair & Track Testing (ETEC)

This gallery showcases the **Rise 5** prototype in its final physical form, documenting both the exhibition and real-world performance validation during the ETEC Industrial Fair.

---

## 🏎️ Track Performance (Videos)
The following footage captures the transition from digital simulation to physical reality.
* **Launch Sequence:** High-speed CO2-powered runs (optimized for 60fps analysis).
* **Aerodynamic Stability:** Visual confirmation of the car's behavior at top speed, validating our CFD data.
* **Pit Operations:** Quick clips of the team preparing the propulsion system and track alignment.

> **Note:** Video files have been compressed to under 16MB to ensure smooth playback directly within the GitHub interface.

## 🖼️ Technical Exhibition (Photos)
Visual evidence of manufacturing quality and structural integrity.

### 🔍 Key Focus Areas:
1. **Technical Scrutineering:** Photographic records of the car undergoing official regulation checks.
2. **Exhibition Stand:** The prototype on display, highlighting the final paint finish and component integration.
3. **Post-Race Analysis:** Close-ups of the chassis and wings after high-speed runs to verify structural durability.

## 📍 Event Context
* **Venue:** ETEC Industrial Fair (Feira Industrial).
* **Primary Objective:** Validating wheelbase alignment, axle friction, and aerodynamic downforce under racing conditions.
