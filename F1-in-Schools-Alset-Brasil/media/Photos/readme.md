# 📸 Industrial Fair & Track Testing (ETEC Showcase)

This gallery documents the **Rise 5** prototype during the ETEC Industrial Fair, focusing on physical validation and real-world track performance.

## 🏎️ Testing Highlights
* **Technical Scrutineering:** Photographic records of the car undergoing pre-race inspections.
* **Track Dynamics:** Action shots of the prototype at peak acceleration on the track.
* **Engineering Exhibition:** The car on display, showcasing final manufacturing quality, paint finish, and component integration.

## 📍 Event Details
* **Location:** ETEC Industrial Fair (Feira Industrial).
* **Objective:** Validating wheelbase alignment, structural integrity after impact, and wheel-to-track friction.
