# 🎥 Performance Demonstration Videos

Short-form video footage capturing the **Rise 5** in action during track testing sessions at the ETEC Industrial Fair.

## 📽️ Visual Content
* **Launch Sequence:** High-speed footage of the CO2-powered start.
* **Aerodynamic Stability:** Visual observation of the car's behavior at top speed (validating CFD data in a real environment).
* **Pit Crew Operations:** Behind-the-scenes clips of the team preparing the propulsion system and lining up the car.

## 🛠️ Test Bench Parameters
* **Track:** Standard F1 in Schools regulation track.
* **Propulsion:** CO2 Power Cartridge.
* **Analysis Focus:** Rear-wing air displacement and axle stability under load.
