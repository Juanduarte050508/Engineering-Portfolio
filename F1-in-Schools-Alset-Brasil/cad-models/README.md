# 📂 CAD Models & 3D Engineering Assets

This folder serves as a gateway to the high-fidelity 3D models developed for the **Rise 5** prototype. 

Due to the high geometric complexity and resolution of the STL meshes (optimized for manufacturing and CFD), the source files are hosted on Google Drive to ensure fast repository performance and easy access.

## 🛠️ Design Specifications
* **Primary Software:** Autodesk Fusion 360
* **Design Philosophy:** Iterative optimization for drag reduction and structural rigidity.
* **Key Files in the Drive:**
    * `full_assembly_rise_5b.stl`: Complete integrated vehicle.
    * `chassis_v20.stl`: Core structural body with aerodynamic refinements.
    * `rear_wing_naca4412.stl`: High-performance wing using NACA 4412 airfoil profile.
    * `manufacturing_jigs.stl`: Custom fixtures designed for precision assembly.

## 📐 Engineering Highlights
The models were developed following a **Modular Design** approach, allowing for rapid testing of different wing profiles and axle configurations. Every component was designed respecting the strict technical regulations of the F1 in Schools competition, focusing on minimum weight requirements and dimensional compliance.
