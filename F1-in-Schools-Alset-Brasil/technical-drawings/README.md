# 📐 Technical Drawings & Manufacturing

Engineering blueprints and precision control documents used to ensure the car's manufacturability and regulatory compliance.

## 📂 Engineering Assets
* **Technical Drawings:** Detailed blueprints of the Rise 5 prototype.
* **Dimensional Control:** Reports ensuring the car meets technical regulations (Scrutineering).
* **Dimensional Tolerance Matrix:** Spreadsheet managing fitment and precision for CNC and 3D printed parts.
* **Manufacturing Jigs:** Technical guides for the 2023-2024 season assembly.
